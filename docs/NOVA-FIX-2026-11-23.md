## 1. Choppy first seconds: what it really was

Before, the browser counted almost every frame as "dropped" from 8 s to 12 s. That happened while she was still hidden
behind the child's full-screen picture: the frames arrived but were not painted. After the reveal, two separate things
held her below 25:
1. **Fewer frames arrived** (16-20 a second): her wide track was simulcast, so the server starts a new viewer on a lower
   layer. `FACE_WIDE_SMOOTH=1` publishes one 720x1280 layer and keeps the frame rate first. Result: **25 frames arrive
   every second from the first second.**
2. **The page paints fewer than arrive**: the browser decoded 25 and showed 16-23. The pod's proof Chrome does all
   drawing in software. Offline measurement (headless Chrome, software compositing): her live picture over the PLAYING
   28 px blurred room behind her gave 20.3 and 21.6 frames a second; with that room PAUSED it gave 24.9. Flag `ambstill`
   holds the blurred room still while her live body is shown. That gained about 1 frame a second on the pod, less than
   offline. **The rest is the proof machine's own painting.**
- `warmgate`: her greeting is asked only after her stream has run at 22 or more frames a second three times in a row.
  On the pod that took 2.0 s (16,25,25,23), so she was never shown on a cold stream.
- `FACE_WIDE_PREP=1`: wide frames are resized and converted in the render thread, not in the 25 fps publish loop.
  Re-anchors fell from 273 to 20.

## 2. Size jump: fixed (`matchcut`)
Measured on the assets themselves (SIFT + RANSAC, 283-398 inliers, `test/matchcut_test.py`): her wide body IS the game
clips' shot zoomed x1.299 about (52.6 %, 42.5 %). This holds for dance-active, dance-sub and idle_sway alike. At the
hand-over the game clip appears with exactly that zoom, so the frames match: mean pixel difference to the wide frame is 3.3
(the old cut was 17.6). Then the camera pulls back to her whole figure in 1.4 s. The ending runs it in reverse: a 1.0 s
push-in, then a hard cut once her live body is moving again. That cut came 3.5 s after the push-in started, at 16 fps
(cap 4 s). There is no crossfade.
