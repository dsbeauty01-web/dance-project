### 2b. Brain: tiny per-step prompt, she may lead
- New `LEAD_CORE` (personality, fixed, ~120 words) + `LEAD_STEP[step]` (goal + what to do with a child question),
  swapped by `session.update` in the safe window. Replaces `V2V_F4_RULES` + `LIVE2_ATTITUDE` + `hear_reply_instr`
  when `NOVA_LEAD=1`. The old blocks stay for flag-off.
- Rule: a child question gets ONE short answer that ends with the step's next action
  ("Every word! Now - what's your name?" / "Yes! Watch your shoulder!").
- Removed for this page: "never offer/start the game", "silence is okay", CONSENT LAW (Freeze is the only game here),
  "ask a question at most every second answer".
- Short real answers are answered, not dropped: 1-word turns ("okay", "yes", "what?") are valid in LEAD steps.
- Free chat (her personality, memory, one question) moves to BETWEEN ROUNDS and the ENDING.

### 2d. Producer protocol (second brain = brain-local.js + page director)
- `outbox` in the bridge: `{fact, t, ttl_ms, cls: 'cue'|'background', say: bool}`.
- Safe window = face server `played_out` for her last line + child silent >= 400 ms + no active response.
  One note per window; expired notes dropped (`lead note dropped: stale`).
- Spoken notes ride as `instructions` on the next `response.create`; silent notes are context. Never a
  `response.create` / `session.update` / item insert while a response is active (local guard, logged).
- `cue` class (Freeze!, animal names, instant praise) = cached clips through the same speech queue; may pre-empt.
- During the intro steps the producer is silent except step lines.

### 2e. Playback truth
- Face server reports `played_out {line_id, ms}` when the last voiced frame of a line is published; brain forwards
  it to the page. Mic gate, repeat rule and notes use it (today: timers + `nova_done`).
