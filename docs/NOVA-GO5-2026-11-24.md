Now each transcript is matched by `item_id`. This also explains part of the "two transcripts disagree" pairs in Refael's 2026-11-23 log.

**Answer time (his words recognised → her voice starts, rig with the face delay simulated at 1.8 s):** FAST_KID on: 2.38-4.58 s, mean 3.35 s (go5b+go5c, 6 answers) · off: 3.19-5.18 s, mean 3.89 s (go5d, 3 answers). ≈ 0.5 s saved; small samples.
