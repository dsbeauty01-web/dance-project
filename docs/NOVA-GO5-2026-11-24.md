## Why
Refael, after his play of nova-go4: "she was monologuing … the game starts with her intro: she asks a name, and the live
shoulder light cue. It's in the repo." nova-go4 kept the light but dropped the conversation: the page staged
`"Hi! I'm Nova, your magical AI dance teacher! Look — a magic light on your shoulder! …"` as one exact 9-second line, and
the brain ran the intro on `FREEZE_RULES` + `INTRO_NOTE` ("NEVER ask their name", "speak only the lines you are given").

## What the approved intro is (sources)
`origin/main` `nova-commercial.html` INTRO-V2V (75396a4, founder spec 2026-09-14, PROMOTE v1.0.4) · `LAWS.md` law-shoulder ·
`pod/rt_lk.py` PROMPT "YOUR INTRO" steps 1-3 · SHOULDER-BEAT §4b (33f83fe, the exact light lines).
1. She greets and asks the name — `Hi! I'm Nova, your magical AI dance teacher! What's your name?` — and stops.
2. The child answers. She gives the exact name back and chats, in her own live words, one line per turn.
3. The name arms the shoulder light; nothing reaches her before it. One 45 s no-name safety.
4. The light lands, she says the approved light line, a real lift wins, or it is released without a claim.
