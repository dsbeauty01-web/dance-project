Privacy Policy

Maya Live

App: livestream (“Maya Live”) · Effective September 3, 2026

Contact: dsbeauty01@gmail.com

What this app is

Maya Live is a live-streaming assistant that operates an AI-powered video host (“Maya”). When connected to a Facebook Page with the Page owner’s permission, it reads viewer comments on that Page’s live broadcasts so the host can respond to them during the stream.

Data we access

With your explicit authorization via Facebook Login, the app accesses: your public profile (name and ID), the list of Pages you manage that you choose to connect, and comments posted on live videos of the connected Page (commenter display name, comment text, and time). We do not access private messages, friend lists, or any data beyond the permissions you approve.

Permissions we request and why

The app requests only the Facebook permissions needed to operate a live broadcast on a Page you own and connect:

  • pages_show_list — to display the list of Pages you manage so you can pick which one to connect. We read only Page names and IDs.
  • pages_read_engagement — to read the viewer comments on the connected Page’s live video (commenter display name, comment text, and time) so the host can respond on stream.
  • pages_manage_posts — to create and end the live video post on the connected Page on your behalf.
  • publish_video — to start the live broadcast (Live Video API) to the connected Page.

We request no other permissions and access no data outside the ones listed above.

How we use it

Live comments are processed in real time solely to display them to the stream operator and to generate the host’s on-stream replies. We do not sell personal data, do not share it with third parties for advertising, and do not use it for any purpose other than operating the live broadcast you connected.

Storage and retention

Comments are processed transiently during a broadcast. Aggregated, non-identifying statistics about a stream (for example, the number of questions answered) may be kept in end-of-stream reports. Access tokens are stored securely and used only to operate the connected Page’s broadcasts.

Data deletion

You can revoke the app’s access at any time from your Facebook settings (Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations), which immediately ends all data access. To request deletion of any data associated with your account or Page, email dsbeauty01@gmail.com with the subject “Data deletion request” and we will delete it within 30 days and confirm by reply.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this same address with a new effective date.
