# TikTok — compliance note (mid-2026)

**Verdict: TikTok is a disclosed *awareness* channel, NOT a live selling channel — for now.
Revisit quarterly.** (Source: live-platform-rules skill + TikTok policy as of 2026-08.)

## In TikTok Shop promotional LIVEs — BANNED
- AI-generated voices / non-real-time verbal interaction (recordings, synthetic speech).
- Enforced via the Account Health Rating (compounding penalties → loss of Shop live access).
- Digital avatars capped at **≤50% of frame**.
- No music on Lives (even royalty-free); live eligibility needs ≥1,000 followers.

→ Maya's format (AI voice, avatar-dominant, scripted) is exactly what Shop LIVEs prohibit.
   Do **not** run Maya as a TikTok Shop live host.

## Outside Shop LIVEs — ALLOWED with discipline
- AI/virtual-avatar content is permitted **with disclosure**: "AI-generated" content label
  on each post + a note in bio.
- **No fabricated testimonials or claims** (same guard as our seller brain — catalog facts
  only). This is both our rule and TikTok's.

## What we CAN do on TikTok today
- Post Maya as **pre-recorded, disclosed** short videos (product explainers, teasers) — the
  loop-mode bakes double as this content, with the AI label already burned into the pixels.
- Drive viewers from those posts to the YouTube/Instagram live where selling actually happens.

## What we must NOT do
- Run her AI voice in a TikTok Shop live.
- Post her without the AI label.
- Let her state any fact not in the catalog.

## Revisit trigger
Re-check this note each quarter (next: 2026-11) — TikTok's AI-in-live stance is changing fast.
