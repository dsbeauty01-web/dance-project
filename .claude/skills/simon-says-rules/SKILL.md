# Simon Says — the real game
## 1 · The real game (what every good "Simon" does)
- **One rule:** move only when the command starts with "Simon says." Everything else is a trap.
- **The leader DEMONSTRATES as they speak.** For young kids every command is shown with the body. On a trick the leader often does the move too — **that's the bait.** The body is never the tell; the words are.
- **Tricks come after momentum.** A good Simon runs 3-4 real commands fast, the kids fall into a rhythm, THEN drops a bare command. A trick first, or tricks spaced evenly, never catches anyone.
- **Cadence:** a natural beat with a short pause after each command — long enough for the move, short enough that the room never goes flat. Too slow = boredom, too fast = confusion. The leader watches the kids and times the next command to them.
- **Commands are short and physical:** touch your head, clap, arms up, jump, freeze, spin, animal moves. One action each (ages 5-7); two-step chains only for older kids.
- **Ages:** 3-4 → no tricks, just "Simon says" + demo. **5-7 → gentle gotchas** (Nova's core). 8+ → speed, chains, sneakier traps.
- **No elimination for kids.** Getting caught is a laugh and you keep playing. Praise the listening, not perfection.
- **The favourite round:** the kid becomes Simon and the leader has to obey.
