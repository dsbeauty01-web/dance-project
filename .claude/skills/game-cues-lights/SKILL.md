# Cues, Lights & Game UX — how a kid knows what to do and that it worked

## Research laws (rhythm games + motion-feedback studies)
1. **Feedback must be IMMEDIATE, PROPORTIONAL, MULTI-SENSORY.** Delayed feedback breaks the sense of control; bigger actions → bigger responses; pair visual + sound (+ voice).
2. **Cue density kills.** "Too many cues in rapid succession" overwhelms — the #1 complaint in motion-feedback studies. One concept at a time; anchor every cue to a beat/event the kid can locate; progressive disclosure (round 1 teaches, later rounds strip hints).
3. **Cues must agree with the ear.** Visual cues land on audio tells already in the music (beats, drops). A cue that fights the sound is noise.
4. **Graded timing windows, not binary.** PERFECT / GOOD / OK inside the window; mistimed early/late spam must not score (hitting everything fast = fail, like every rhythm game).
5. **Instructor-demo + approaching-cue mix works** (both modalities tested equal; mixing is fine). For kids: discrete feedback (stars/bins), not numbers-heavy.
6. **External focus in cue wording** (OPTIMAL theory): cue the TARGET ("reach the light!", "chase the gold") — never the body part ("move your shoulder"). Internal-focus cues freeze motor learning.

## Nova's light language (the signature — one color glows)
- **Gold = the only action color.** Purple = chrome/structure, room-pink = ambient, ice-blue = freeze only. Never red, never punishing.
- **Orb** pulses on the kid's LIVE joint one beat early (follows their real shoulder) · **ring** closes toward the orb = the window countdown · **ribbon** leans the direction · **flare + sparkles + score fly-up** on hit, tick pitch rises with streak · **soft fade** on miss · **hip gold-shimmer** for true isolation · **fire mode** at streak ≥3 (orb turns flame, ×2 shown) · **warm** (orange, soft) for wobble — never red.
- Lights render on a canvas over the mirrored usercam; joint positions from the live keypoints every frame.
- Cue lead: slow rounds 1.2s, fast 0.8s. Arrows/letters only in round 1's first loop, then the light carries it.

## Layout laws
- Talking phases (intro/between/ending): Nova wider (~66/34), calm body, lips. Game rounds: 60/40 — her routine/body left as the clock, the KID's side hot (all lights + score live on them). Adaptive width transition 0.5s.
- Full figure, never cropped (LAW-FRAME-FULL); ambient room-stretch fills margins (blurred self-copy scaleX 2.2, blur 28px); zero black anywhere.
- Persistent: state badge (Watching/Listening/Talking/FREEZE!), Pause + Exit always visible, round dots, score tabular-nums, "Nova's warming up ✨" loading state.
- Type: display Baloo 2 700-800, body Nunito 600. HUD top-center, never over faces.

## Juice checklist (every hit must have all three)
visual (flare/ring/fly-up) · audio (tick, pitch-scaled) · voice or badge (fact-fed, ≤6 words, not every hit — ≤1 line/gap)

## Anti-patterns
Two cues in one beat · cues before the music's beat grid · red anything · countdown bars replacing the ring · numbers instead of stars for ages 4-8 · cropped avatar · hints that never fade.
